Bridgeway exists because medical freight is not parcel freight. A general courier optimises for stops per hour, which is the correct way to move packages and the wrong way to move a specimen with a stability window. Consolidating a cooler onto a multi-stop van adds hours, hand-offs and an unrecorded gap in the custody trail.

So the model here is narrow on purpose. One courier owns a run start to finish. Nothing is consolidated with unrelated freight. The manifest closes with a signature and a timestamp, and you can have a copy the same day. That is the whole product.

Driver standards

What every courier clears before they carry anything, and what stays on file for your vendor packet.

  1. 01

    Criminal background check and driving record

    Run before a courier is assigned a first pickup, not after. Motor vehicle records are reviewed for moving violations and licence status, and re-checked annually.

  2. 02

    HIPAA training, with a certificate on file

    Covering protected health information in transit: what may be discussed, what may not be photographed, and how a document case is handled when a vehicle is left. Repeated annually. Certificates go to your compliance file on request.

  3. 03

    Bloodborne pathogen and spill handling

    Containment and reporting procedure for a leaking or damaged container, including what gets sealed, who gets called and in what order.

  4. 04

    Cold-chain handling

    Correct container for each temperature range, how long a validated cooler holds under real conditions, and what to do when a reading drifts out of range mid-run.

  5. 05

    Employees, not subcontractors

    No gig platform drivers and no brokered runs. The person who arrives at your facility works for Bridgeway and has cleared everything above.

  6. 06

    Insurance certificate issued to you

    Commercial auto, general liability and cargo coverage. We issue a certificate of insurance naming your facility, and send a renewal copy before it lapses without waiting to be asked.

Ask the hard questions on the phone.

Insurance limits, training records, how a missed pickup gets handled. Call and ask. If the answers work for your compliance file, we can talk about a route.
