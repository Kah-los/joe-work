The rules this work runs under

Four bodies of regulation govern medical courier work here. The citations are public and verifiable. What Bridgeway holds against each is stated separately and plainly.

45 CFR Part 164

HIPAA Privacy and Security Rules

A courier handling protected health information is generally a business associate, which means a signed business associate agreement, safeguards for information in transit, and breach notification duties that run back to your facility.

Bridgeway signs your BAA and trains every courier on PHI in transit annually.

29 CFR 1910.1030

OSHA Bloodborne Pathogens Standard

Anyone with reasonably anticipated occupational exposure to blood or other potentially infectious material needs a written exposure control plan, annual training, protective equipment, and a defined spill and exposure procedure.

Bridgeway maintains an exposure control plan, annual training, and a spill kit in every vehicle.

49 CFR 173.199

DOT rules for Category B infectious substances

Diagnostic specimens moved as UN 3373 Category B require triple packaging: a leakproof primary receptacle, leakproof secondary packaging with absorbent material, and a rigid outer container marked with the UN 3373 diamond and proper shipping name.

Bridgeway carries compliant outer containers and will not accept a specimen that is incorrectly packaged at origin.

42 CFR Part 493

CLIA specimen integrity

Your laboratory's CLIA obligations include written criteria for specimen acceptance and rejection. A transport failure, whether a broken temperature range or an exceeded stability window, becomes your rejection and your redraw.

Bridgeway reports any temperature excursion or delay on the manifest rather than leaving you to find it at accession.

What goes into your procurement file

The documents a healthcare vendor packet normally asks for. Ask once and you get the set, without chasing.

  • W-9
  • HIPAA training certificate per courier
  • Bloodborne pathogen training records
  • Written exposure control and spill procedure
  • Background check attestation per courier
  • Sample chain-of-custody manifest
  • Temperature log sample from a completed run
  • References from current accounts
