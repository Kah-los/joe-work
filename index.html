What you are vetting

The questions a compliance file has to answer, answered before you ask.

HIPAA trained

Every courier trains on protected health information in transit, and re-trains annually. Certificates go into your vendor file on request.

Bonded and insured

Commercial auto, general liability and cargo coverage. We issue the certificate directly to your facility, named to your entity.

Drivers checked

Criminal background check and motor vehicle record review before a courier carries a first specimen. No gig or brokered drivers.

Cold chain held

Validated containers for refrigerated and frozen transport, temperature logged for the whole run and reported with the manifest.

Four lines of work

If your run does not fit one of them, call and describe it.

Emergency and STAT runs

One dedicated courier, one pickup, one drop. Nothing else on the vehicle and no route deviation. For time-critical specimens, blood products and anything with a stability window measured in hours.

Dispatched on call

Scheduled lab routes

Recurring pickups in a fixed window, daily or on the days you choose. The same courier on your route wherever possible, so your front desk deals with someone they know by name.

Fixed daily window

Cold-chain pharma

Refrigerated and frozen transport in validated containers, monitored for the whole run, with the temperature log delivered alongside the manifest. For specialty pharmacy, infusion and trial material.

Temperature logged

Records and equipment

Medical records, imaging media, signed consents and small equipment, moved under the same custody documentation as specimens. Sealed transfer, signature on delivery.
