Chain of custody, every run

  1. Pickup

    Timestamped at the collection point. Container sealed and the seal number written to the manifest.

  2. Transit

    One courier, start to finish. No hand-offs, no shared load, no warehouse stop.

  3. Delivery

    Signature captured at the receiving department and timestamped against the pickup.

  4. Record

    The completed manifest is retained and sent to you on request, same day.

What you are vetting

The questions a compliance file has to answer, answered up front.

HIPAA trained
Every courier completes HIPAA training covering protected health information in transit, and re-trains annually. Certificates available for your vendor file.
Bonded and insured
Commercial auto, general liability and cargo coverage. Certificate of insurance issued directly to your facility, with your entity named on request.
Background-checked drivers
Criminal background check and motor vehicle record review before a courier carries a first specimen. No subcontracted or gig drivers.
Cold-chain capable
Validated containers for refrigerated and frozen transport, with temperature logged for the duration of the run and reported with the manifest.

Services

Four lines of work. If your run does not fit one of them, call and describe it.

  • STAT

    Emergency and STAT runs

    A single dedicated courier dispatched to one pickup and one drop. Nothing else on the vehicle, no route deviation. For time-critical specimens, blood products and anything with a stability window measured in hours.

    Dispatched on call
  • ROUTE

    Scheduled lab routes

    Recurring pickups at a fixed time window, daily or on the days you choose. Same courier on your route wherever possible, so your front desk deals with a person they know.

    Fixed daily window
  • COLD

    Cold-chain pharma transport

    Refrigerated and frozen transport in validated containers, with temperature monitored for the whole run and the log delivered alongside the manifest. For specialty pharmacy, infusion and clinical trial material.

    Temperature logged
  • DOC

    Records and equipment

    Medical records, imaging media, signed consents and small equipment moved under the same custody documentation as specimens. Sealed transfer, signature on delivery.

    Same day
